# Spec graph layout

## Regions

Repository navigation, page heading and the revision line precede the reader. The specification tree sits beside the graph on wide screens and collapses behind a toggle under 1024px. Above the canvas sit the highlight field with its match count and the group toggles with node counts. The canvas has zoom in, zoom out and fit controls in its top-right corner and a legend in its bottom-left corner. From 1280px the selected-node panel sits to the right of the canvas; below that it follows the canvas.
